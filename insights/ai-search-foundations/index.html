AI search: keep the foundations, test the hypotheses

Distinguish published guidance from experimental discoverability ideas.

Educational article · Published 24 October 2026 · Sources checked 24 October 2026 · 2 min read

Start with what is established

Google states that its existing SEO foundations remain relevant to AI Overviews and AI Mode. Eligible supporting pages must be indexed and able to appear in Search with a snippet. Google does not require a special AI schema or new AI-specific text file for these features.

Keep the scope honest

That guidance concerns Google’s named features. It does not establish how every other assistant retrieves or cites websites. Avoid combining different products into a single “AI ranking” metric or claiming that one markup change guarantees inclusion.

Build an experiment register

For an emerging tactic, write the hypothesis, the pages affected, the intended mechanism and the observation window. Separate changes that improve reader comprehension from claims about an external system’s selection behavior. Record what could disprove the hypothesis before starting.

Use cautious observation

If you sample answers manually, record the exact query, product, date, location assumptions and whether the answer links to your page. Repeated prompts can produce different answers. A handful of appearances is an observation, not a causal estimate or a forecast of business impact.

Keep business questions central

Useful content, clear ownership and reliable measurement remain practical priorities. Treat GEO and AIO as areas for disciplined learning, not guaranteed visibility packages. This article is an educational analysis; it does not claim personal AI-search ranking improvements.
